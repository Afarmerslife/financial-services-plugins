# SEC Filings Reference — Debt Financing & M&A Transactions (2024–2026)

description: Reference catalog of notable SEC EDGAR filings for debt financing prospectuses and M&A merger proxy statements from the past two years. Use as examples when drafting CIMs, merger models, pitch decks, or process letters. Triggers on "SEC filings", "debt offering examples", "M&A filing examples", "prospectus examples", "merger proxy examples", "EDGAR reference", or "transaction filings".

---

## Important Note on Document Types

**What IS on SEC EDGAR:**
- Prospectus supplements (424B2/424B5) for public debt offerings
- Merger proxy statements (DEFM14A) and registration statements (S-4)
- 8-K announcements of deals, debt offerings, and closings
- Tender offer statements (SC TO-T, SC 13E3)

**What is NOT on SEC EDGAR:**
- Confidential Information Memorandums (CIMs) — proprietary sell-side documents
- M&A pitch books / transaction decks — internal investment banking materials
- Debt financing memorandums — private placement / bank book documents
- Fairness opinion presentations (internal) — only the opinion letter itself appears in proxies

Use the public filings below as structural references and data sources for building proprietary deal materials.

## Part 1: Major Debt Financing — SEC Prospectus Filings (2024–2026)

### 1.1 AbbVie Inc. — $15 Billion Senior Notes (February 2024)

- **Filing type:** 424B5 Prospectus Supplement
- **Date:** February 22, 2024
- **Amount:** $15.0 billion aggregate principal across multiple tranches
- **Purpose:** Fund acquisitions of ImmunoGen Inc. and Cerevel Therapeutics Holdings Inc.
- **SEC EDGAR:** https://investors.abbvie.com/sec-filings (CIK: 1551152)
- **Key details:**
  - One of the largest pharma bond offerings in history
  - Multiple tranches with maturities ranging from 2027 to 2054
  - As of December 31, 2024, AbbVie had $64.8B aggregate principal of outstanding unsecured senior notes
  - Proceeds used for M&A financing — classic acquisition-driven debt issuance
- **Advisors:** Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, BofA Securities (bookrunners)
- **Why relevant:** Exemplifies acquisition-financing debt structure with multi-tranche offering

## Part 2B: Small Transactions Under $50 Million — SEC Filings (2024–2026)

These smaller deals are harder to find on EDGAR because they receive less media coverage and many small acquirers are not required to file 8-Ks for immaterial acquisitions. Below are confirmed transactions with SEC filings.

### 2B.1 CFSB Bancorp / Hometown Financial Group — $44 Million

- **Filing type:** DEFM14A (CFSB Bancorp)
- **Announcement date:** May 20, 2025
- **Closing date:** November 1, 2025
- **Deal value:** ~$44 million ($14.25/share, all-cash)
- **Sector:** Community banking
- **Key details:**
  - CFSB had $366.6M total assets, $75.7M shareholders' equity at time of merger
  - Hometown Financial ($6.6B assets) acquired CFSB's Colonial Federal Savings Bank
  - Hometown's eighth strategic merger in the last decade
  - Stock moved from $8.19 to $13.88 after announcement
  - Special meeting held September 16, 2025; regulatory approvals October 21, 2025
  - Termination fee: $1.6 million
- **Advisors:** Piper Sandler (financial); Kilpatrick Townsend (legal, buyer); Luse Gorman (legal, seller)
- **Why relevant:** Classic small community bank merger with full DEFM14A; clean example of sub-$50M deal process

### 2B.2 Century Therapeutics / Clade Therapeutics — $45 Million

## Part 3: How to Use These Filings

### For CIM Drafting
- Review DEFM14A filings for company descriptions, industry overviews, and financial summaries used in "Background of the Merger" sections
- Extract comparable transaction multiples from fairness opinions embedded in proxy statements
- Use prospectus risk factors as templates for industry-specific risk language

### For Merger Models
- S-4 filings contain pro forma financial statements showing combined entity projections
- DEFM14A fairness opinions include DCF assumptions, comparable companies, and precedent transactions
- 8-K filings provide final deal terms and closing adjustments

### For Pitch Decks
- Precedent transaction data from the deals above for transaction comps slides
- Premium analysis data (e.g., Kellanova 44% premium to VWAP, Discover 26.6% premium)
- Financing structure examples from debt prospectuses

### For Debt Financing Memorandums
- 424B2/424B5 filings provide structural templates: risk factors, use of proceeds, description of notes
- Multi-tranche structures from AbbVie ($15B, 8 tranches) and Broadcom ($5B, 3 tranches)
- Acquisition financing language from Synopsys debt commitment structure

### For Process Letters
- DEFM14A "Background of the Merger" sections detail the auction/negotiation process
- Timeline references from announcement to close (e.g., Capital One/Discover: 15 months)
- Regulatory milestone tracking (FTC Second Requests, state approvals)
